## Executive summary

ALO is a family-run Lancashire soft play hire business whose supplied Facebook Group
description serves children under five and names birthdays, weddings, christenings,
indoor/outdoor events, Stay & Play and community events. It already has a usable brand
line: **“Little people 🧸 Big memories.”** It states that bookings and enquiries can go
through messages or `alosoftplayhirelancs@outlook.com`.

The pilot should make it easier for a parent or event organiser to understand the offer,
see the real equipment, check delivery coverage and send a useful availability enquiry.
The owner is busy; collect only the launch-critical answers first. Digital confidence
should shape a simple handover and support plan, not become a negative label in the report.

The strongest positioning hypotheses to test are community warmth, convenient party hire,
and the relationship between private hire and Stay & Play. The screenshots also contain a
fundraising statement about Derian House; verify its scope and the owner's preferred wording.
These are promising angles, not proven market exclusivity or formal partnership claims.

## 4. Foundations and trust, with applicability

1. Verify what accounts/sites already exist, then decide whether a dedicated Facebook Page
   alongside the Group and a clear landing page would reduce enquiry friction.
2. Consider a Google service-area profile only after confirming eligibility and actual
   operations. [Google's guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en)
   says businesses that do not serve customers at their business address should remove
   that address from the public profile. Do not display a home address by default.
3. Show truthful, owner-confirmed equipment, cleaning, insurance and supervision information.
   Credentials advertised by competitors are not automatically legal requirements for ALO.
4. [HSE's inflatable guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/entertainment/fairgrounds/inflatables.htm)
   explicitly ties PIPA to inflatable devices within the relevant standard's scope. Do not
   demand a generic “PIPA-certified” badge for non-inflatable foam soft play. Confirm the
   actual inventory and equipment-specific requirements first. DBS and first-aid questions
   also need the operator's role and session model established.
5. Confirm the Derian House statement and any badge/logo permissions before publishing.

This pilot is about clear customer information and the right foundations. It is not a
reason to sell every channel, invent a compliance failure or promise immediate bookings.

## 6. Initial website brief

- Real kit setup as the hero, the owner's brand line, and a clear enquiry action.
- Packages with actual contents/prices or an honest agreed quote-based approach.
- Confirmed towns/postcodes, delivery/setup/collection details and space/age requirements.
- Stay & Play as a separate section only when there are confirmed sessions or an accurate
  interest-list proposition; no invented dates, availability or booking inventory.
- Owner-approved trust information, practical FAQs and a proportionate enquiry flow: date,
  event postcode, occasion, chosen package, contact details and relevant questions.
- Fundraising information only in confirmed wording. No charity logo or partnership badge
  is assumed. No invented reviews, stock photos presented as ALO's kit or generated families.
- Visual direction follows the actual logo and equipment; “pastel luxury” is not presumed.
  Socialite's signature should come through composition, typography, image treatment and
  mobile usefulness, while the page remains recognisably ALO.

## 7. Pilot success measures

Record the baseline first, then assess whether customers can understand packages/coverage,
send an enquiry successfully and whether the owner spends less time answering repeated
questions. Track enquiry quality and response effort, not just visits or follower counts.
Testimonials/case-study permission, pricing, ongoing support and ownership terms remain
separate discussions; friendship does not decide them automatically.
