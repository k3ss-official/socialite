## First: just the information needed for an accurate draft

1. **What can someone book today?** Send your packages/price list if you have one. For each:
   what is included, hire duration, price and whether delivery, setup and collection are
   included. If you prefer quotes rather than public prices, say so.
2. **Where do you cover?** Your base town and the towns/postcode areas you serve, plus any
   travel charges. We do not need to display your home address. What floor space is needed,
   and are any kits indoor-only or weather-dependent?
3. **What should we show?** Your logo and a few photos of your own kit set up. Confirm which
   images we may use and whether you own them or have permission. Equipment-only photos are
   sufficient. Any preferred colours or example sites you like?
4. **Where should an enquiry go?** Confirm the email above, the exact Facebook Group link and
   any existing Page/Instagram/website/Google profile. Do you want email, Messenger, WhatsApp
   or another channel as the main route? Is the business taking bookings now?
5. **What practical reassurance can we accurately state?** Insurance relevant to the service,
   cleaning routine, checks appropriate to your equipment, and who supervises children.
   No need to send sensitive policy documents in a group chat; tell us the wording you can
   support and how we should confirm it privately.
6. **Which extras are live?** Are Stay & Play sessions already running—where, when and how are
   they booked? What exactly is the Derian House fundraising arrangement, and what wording
   are you happy for us to use? We will not imply an official partnership or use their logo
   without confirmation.

## Later, before publishing or enabling bookings

- Deposit/payment, cancellation/rescheduling/weather terms, booking confirmation process and
  realistic enquiry response times. No automatic “available” calendar until there is a
  dependable availability source and the owner agrees to maintain it.
- Contact/trading details appropriate for the site, approved business story and optional
  public owner introduction. No unrelated personal profile or family information is needed.
- Rules and equipment requirements for each service; responsibility differences between
  equipment hire and owner-run sessions; appropriate evidence for published credentials.
- Approved claims, current prices, image permissions, privacy information and enquiry delivery.
- What the owner wants help with after launch, who maintains changes, commercial terms and
  any separate permission for a testimonial or case study.

## How answers enter the Bible

Keep each answer's source, received date, speaker/role and any qualification. Mark it
**owner-stated** until reviewed, rather than describing it as independently certified.
Confirm conflicting information with the owner. Keep private operating details and policy
documents separate from the public page projection. Staff approval and eventual publication
approval remain explicit; participation in the pilot does not approve every claim or asset.
